Support & FAQ

Is this a real online store?

No. Products, prices, cart, orders and forms are for learning only.

Will checkout charge my card?

No payment gateway is connected. Do not enter real payment details.

Does installing GTM mean GA4 is configured?

No. The container snippet loads GTM; tags, triggers, variables, account linking and consent settings must be configured separately.

Does a download event prove someone read the file?

No. It tracks a download click/initiated download, not confirmed file consumption.

Where are demo form submissions sent?

They are not sent to a server. The form displays local success feedback and pushes a demo event to the browser dataLayer.
